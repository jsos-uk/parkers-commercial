FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key considerations for commercial gutter maintenance and block drainage.

How often should commercial property gutters be cleared?

Commercial gutters should be inspected and cleared at least once or twice annually (typically late autumn and early spring) to prevent overflows, water ingress, and structural dampness.

How high can your commercial gutter vacuum systems reach?

Our carbon-fibre vacuum poles reach up to 50 feet (approx. 4 storeys) safely from ground level. For higher or complex architectural profiles, our IPAF-certified operators utilize MEWPs and rope access systems.

How do you assess commercial gutters before carrying out work?

We carry out an initial site assessment to evaluate your building's height, roof perimeter, and access routes. From there, we deploy the most cost-effective and safe access method tailored to your facility.

Do you service property managers and residential blocks across London & Essex?

Yes, we regularly contract with block management companies, managing agents, and multi-academy trusts across London, Romford, Brentwood, Chelmsford, Colchester, and Southend-on-Sea.
